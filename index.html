Straight answers

The questions everybody asks

Who actually owns the domain?
You do. It goes in your name, with your details, and I will give you the login. If you ever want to move it somewhere else, or hand it to someone cheaper than me, you can, and I will help you do it. Holding people's domains hostage is how web people get a bad name.
What happens after the first year?
It goes to £49 a year, which covers renewing your domain, keeping the site online, and small changes through the year like a new phone number, new prices or new opening hours. Cancel whenever you like. You keep the address either way.
Is this just a template with my name dropped in?
No, and you can prove it yourself further up this page. What goes on the page, what order it goes in, and what it looks like all come out of what you tell me about the business. A roofer and a wedding florist need genuinely different pages, so they get them. What is the same underneath is the engineering, which is why it costs ninety nine quid instead of two grand.
Who writes the words?
On the £199 one, I do, from what you tell me. And here is the important bit: nothing goes on your site that you did not tell me. No invented years of experience, no qualifications you haven't got, no awards. You read it before it goes live and I would rather sound plain than have you explaining a made up claim to a customer.
What if I pick a look and then go off it?
Tell me and I will switch it. Changing the style or the colour is about a minute of work, because the site is built to do exactly that. I am not going to charge you or sigh down the phone.
Do I get an email address like hello@mybusiness.co.uk?
You get forwarding, included, so anything sent to hello@yourname.co.uk drops straight into the inbox you already use. It works perfectly and it costs nothing. A proper mailbox you can send from is about £70 a year on top, and most sole traders genuinely do not need one. Ask me and I will tell you honestly whether you do.
What don't you do?
Online shops, booking and payment systems, membership areas, anything with a login. Not because they are difficult, but because they are not ninety nine quid and I would rather say so now than halfway through. If that is what you need, tell me and I will point you at someone good.
